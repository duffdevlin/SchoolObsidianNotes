1. The first step in designing an electrochemical cell is determining the species of which you are attempting to reduce/oxidize. Finding the standard reduction potentials, you can determine the actual reduction or oxidation potentials for the conditions of each half-cell using the Nernst equation. Once the real half-cell potentials have been calculated, they can be added to give you the real cell potential, and whether it will be [[Galvanic Cells|galvanic]] or [[Electrolytic Cells|electrolytic]].
