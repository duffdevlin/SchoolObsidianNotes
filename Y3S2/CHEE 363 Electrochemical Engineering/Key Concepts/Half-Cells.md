Half-cells are theoretical constructs where an oxidation or reduction reaction occurs in isolation. Each half-cell reaction can be combined with another to form an electrochemical reaction. This can then be used in electrochemical processes. 

When looking at a list of half-cell reactions, the reduction potential will be listed next to it. The oxidation potential of a half-cell reaction is the negative of whatever the reduction potential is, and the sum of these values determine whether the full cell is electrolytic or galvanic.
