A wavefunction in one dimension depends on the single spatial dimension as well as changing over time, giving a function $\psi(x,t)$. This function is defined by a cosine wave, given a angular frequency, $\omega$, and a wavenumber, k. This combined with a phase shift, representing the initial conditions, $\phi$, gives the one spatial dimension function shown below with amplitude $A$.
$$\psi(x,t)=A\cdot cos(kx-\omega t+\psi)$$
