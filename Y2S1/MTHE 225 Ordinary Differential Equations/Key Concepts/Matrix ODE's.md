The solution to a matrix ODE in the form:
$$\vec{A}\vec{x}=\vec{x}'$$
is solved by finding the [[Eigenvectors|eigenvector]] pairs, $\vec{a}\pm\vec{b}i$, and corresponding [[Eigenvalues|eigenvalues]], $\alpha\pm\beta i$, in the form of a linear combination of the below equations.
$$e^{\alpha t}[cos(\beta t)\vec{a}-sin(\beta t)\vec{b}]$$
$$e^{\alpha t}[sin(\beta t)\vec{a}+cos(\beta t)\vec{b}]$$
