Point groups are symbols that describe the possible [[Molecular Symmetry Operations|symmetry operations]] that can be performed on a molecule and still return an indistinguishable form from the original. After a point group has been identified, you can look at their respective [[Character Tables|character table]] identifying the [[Irreducible Representation|irreducible symmetry operations]] that can be performed on the molecule. The [[Irreducible Representation|irreducible representations]] are used in identifying [[Atomic Orbitals|atomic]] and [[Ligand-Group Orbitals|ligand-group]] orbitals that can contribute to the same [[Advanced Molecular Orbitals|molecular orbitals]], as well as identifying [[Raman Activity|Raman]] and [[Infrared Activity|IR]] activity.
