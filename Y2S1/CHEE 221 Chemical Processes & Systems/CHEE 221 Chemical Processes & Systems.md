Introduction to the fundamentals of chemical engineering, analyzing how to find all process variables from a limited set, and whether a [[Steady-State Solutions|steady-state]] can be found by simple algebraic analysis. The key concepts shown below give you the tools to analyze systems with [[Recycle Streams|recycle]], [[Bypass Streams|bypass]], and [[Purge Streams|purge]] streams, as well as single and multi-[[Process Units|unit]] processes. For more advanced problems within this course, both material and energy balances will be used together.
