Energy balances look at the energy in a system, both the [[Internal Energy|internal energy]], a combination of [[Enthalpy|enthalpy]], pressure, and volume. This is in addition to [[Energy Balance Gravitational Potential|potential energy]], [[Energy Balance Kinetic Energy|kinetic energy]], and [[Energy Balance Work|work]] done on or by the system. The general form of the energy balance is shown below:
$$\Delta E=\Delta U+\Delta E_k+\Delta E_p-Q-W=\Delta H-\Delta (PV) +\Delta E_k+\Delta E_p-Q-W$$
This is made discrete in the case of [[Closed Systems|closed systems]], but would just comprise of [[Differentials|differential]] forms with respect to time in the case of an [[Open System|open system]]. The terms for [[Energy Balance Work|work]] $W$ and [[Energy Balance Heat|heat]] $Q$ transferred have been formalized as energy from outside the system moving into the system. This is important to be consistent with, as treating it in an incorrect manner would result in issues such as a mixer lowering energy in the system, even though it should increase the [[Energy Balance Kinetic Energy|kinetic energy]].

The [[Energy Balance Kinetic Energy|kinetic]] and [[Energy Balance Gravitational Potential|potential]] energy terms follow the equations for kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy as normal, with mass flow rates instead of a constant mass. Tables must be used for values such as [[Specific Properties|specific]] [[Enthalpy|enthalpy]], [[Internal Energy|internal energy]], and [[Specific Properties|specific]] volume at various conditions to solve problems with these values.

When looking at the tabulated values for [[Enthalpy|enthalpy]], it must be kept in mind that sometimes it may not be possible to go directly from one state to another, and therefore [[Hess's Law|Hess's law]] must be used to calculate intermediate values, all contributing to the total change in [[Enthalpy|enthalpy]].

The [[Enthalpy|enthalpy]] or [[Internal Energy|internal energy]] term in the balance must account for all species present in the system, with each component able to have their own declared [[Reference States|reference state]], so long as the total change is taken and summed, the calculations will work.
